Synthesis Reasoning

Mexico's case is almost entirely context-based rather than quality-based. Synthesis Reasoning

Brazil open against Morocco at MetLife on June 13 — by far the group's defining fixture. The opening result will shape everything that follows. Morocco are built to beat exactly this type of opponent — compact shape, Hakimi's overlapping runs as a release valve, Brahim Diaz picking pockets between the lines.
